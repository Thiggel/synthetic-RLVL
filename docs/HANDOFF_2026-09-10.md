## 2. Two clusters, and why work moved

**alex** is at a hard, account-wide **24-GPU cap** (`AssocGrpGRES`). The three
30-percent midtrain arms hold exactly 24 GPUs, so *everything else there is
blocked*, including one-GPU evaluation jobs. There is no CPU escape: a
`--gres=gpu:CPU-only:1` job still counts against the cap, and a job with no
`--gres` is rejected outright ("Jobs on Alex must always allocate at least one
GPU"). A root-owned maintenance reservation, **REINST-CONFLUENT**, runs
2026-09-08 to **2026-12-16** over 56 nodes and is why queue waits are 34-53 h
and why rtxpro6k is unusable (ten of its eleven nodes are inside it).

**gruenau** is the escape hatch and is now a full second cluster for this work.
Slurm, 14-day walltimes, no cap.

| node | GPUs | partition | used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| gruenau11 | 4x H100 (2 NVL 96 GB, 2 PCIe 82 GB) | `gpu-staff` | instruction tuning |
| gruenau9 | 3x A100 80 GB | `gpu` | conversions |
| gruenau7, gruenau8 | 4x RTX A6000 48 GB each | `gpu` | evaluation |

The filesystems are **separate**; everything must be copied. Transfer runs at
44-80 MB/s, so a 15 GB checkpoint is about 6 minutes and a 29 GB one about 11.

### gruenau environment, and its traps

- `~/.venv_rlvl_gruenau` — training. **torch must stay 2.9.0+cu128.** Torch
  2.11 makes this exact script diverge instantly (loss 4.7e10, then NaN, then
  loss 0.0). The weights were fine; it was the torch version. Everything else
  is pinned to alex: transformers 4.57.3, trl 0.24.0, peft 0.17.1,
  accelerate 1.13.0.
- `~/.venv_rlvl_gruenau_eval` — evaluation, isolated so an install cannot
  disturb a running training job. vllm 0.11.1, lm_eval 0.4.11, torch 2.9.0,
  transformers 4.57.3, matching alex exactly so numbers are comparable.
- `~/nanotron_src` — the patched nanotron checkout, installed `-e`.
  Conversion needs `PYTHONPATH=~/nanotron_src` and must run under
  `torchrun --standalone --nproc_per_node=1`, or it dies on
  `KeyError: 'WORLD_SIZE'`.
- **A conversion-only flash-attention stub** lives in the eval-free training
  venv's site-packages. nanotron imports flash-attn at module scope and
  building the real thing takes hours. Every symbol raises when called, so
  conversion (which never runs attention) works and anything else fails loudly.
  Do not mistake it for a real install.
- **gruenau GPUs are shareable (MPS).** A neighbour can hold most of a card.
  `gruenau_full_eval.slurm` now waits up to two hours for free memory and sizes
  `gpu_memory_utilization` to what is actually free. Six jobs died before this
  guard existed.
- **Interconnect:** gruenau11 NVLinks GPU0-1 and GPU2-3 but joins the pairs
  only over host PCIe, so ZeRO-3 is expensive. Measured seconds per optimizer
  step: 2 GPUs batch 1 = 210, 4 GPUs batch 1 = 110, **4 GPUs batch 2 = 91
  (used)**, batch 4 = OOM, ZeRO-2 = OOM. Alex does the same at ~18 s/step.

## 3. Results that are settled

**The corrected sampled readout.** The 2026-09-07 sampled numbers were an
artifact: the sampler passed no `stop_token_ids`, so `<|im_end|>` never
stopped generation, every sample ran to the 2048-token cap, and last-match

`qwen25_longwin_passk_20260908_eosfix`. **The sign reversed.** Pooled pass@16,
both seeds: Control 57.0/57.4, LongDoc 61.4/57.5, Formal 81.4/82.0, English
86.4/86.9. English minus Formal is +5.0 and +4.9. Never cite the old root.

**Everything in the paper replicates across two instruction-tuning seeds.**
Mean absolute difference 0.015 over 50 condition-task pairs, max 0.050.
ProofWriter depth 3: English 57.0/57.0, Formal 50.0/50.0, Control 44.4/43.4.

**The LogiQA cost is real, not noise.** Derivation arms lose 2-3 points to
control in both seeds and on both the raw and length-normalised metric. Stated
in the abstract, results and conclusion. Do not quietly drop it.

## 7. Goal 5: making the ProofWriter gain referee-proof

### Done, and strong

- **Calibration objection is dead.** Matthews correlation, which is invariant
  to a shift in answer prior, goes Control 0.095 -> English 0.363, replicated
  at 0.075 -> 0.363. Balanced accuracy 0.367 -> 0.455, macro-F1 0.317 -> 0.396.
- **Significance.** Paired McNemar p = 5e-39 and 8e-46 for English vs Control;
  bootstrap over items [8.7, 11.8] and [9.5, 12.6] points. **LongDoc vs Control
  p = 0.62 and 1.00** — the length-matched control is indistinguishable from
  control, which is the cleanest available statement that the intervention's
  size is not what matters.
- **Not a threshold shift.** English gains 228 gold-false items and loses 29
  gold-true. Accuracy on false rises 29.5 points while true falls 2.
- **Contamination ruled out.** Zero of 2500 ProofWriter items share even one
  8-gram with the training derivations. Top-300 vocabulary overlap is 4 words
  (1.3 percent); "the", "not", "someone", "rabbit", "tiger" never appear in
  training at all. Transfer crosses an almost lexically disjoint boundary.
- **Depth profile behaves.** English minus Control: +4.6 at depth 0 (lookup),
  then +6.0, +12.2, +12.6, +10.4.
- Scripts: `scripts/analysis/proofwriter_rigor.py`,
  `scripts/analysis/contamination_check.py`.

### Done, and a complication to be honest about

**FOLIO (out-of-family, human-written FOL, 203 items, near-balanced labels).**
Control 55.2, LongDoc 56.2, Formal 60.1, English 60.1. Both derivation arms
+4.9, control arm +1.0. Direction replicates but the magnitude is roughly a
third of ProofWriter's, and **Formal and English tie**, whereas English led
Formal by 7 on ProofWriter. Reading: part of the ProofWriter margin is genre
affinity. 4.9 points on 203 items will not clear significance alone — compute
the paired test and run seed 3408 before this goes in the paper.

### Still to do

1. **Paired statistics for FOLIO** (McNemar + bootstrap), both seeds. Cheap,
   from stored samples. Do this first.
2. **GPQA-Diamond rerun.** Every pre-2026-09-10 GPQA number is **void**: the
   8-token cap truncated ~30 percent of responses mid-explanation. Task now
   allows 4096 tokens, invites brief working, and extracts the last
   "Answer: X". Expect it to stay near or below the 25 percent chance line at
   7B — it is a safety check that nothing was damaged, not evidence of gain.
   Canonical GPQA is gated; we use the `fingertap/GPQA-Diamond` mirror, so
   absolute numbers are not comparable to published scores but the
   control-versus-treatment contrast is.
3. **Base-versus-instruction-tuned comparison** (§4, `KIND=base`). Does the
   midtrained model still emit formal derivations before instruction tuning
   washes them out? If yes, run the proof checker on those generations: that
   would be the first checker-selection result on a real midtrained model and
   the paper's missing link.
4. **Dose-response** from the 30-percent arms.
5. **A scrambled-derivation control**, if there is compute. Same corpus with
   proof steps shuffled so token statistics survive but the logic does not. If
   that arm does not transfer, the gain is due to valid deduction rather than
   surface structure. This is the tightest control available and costs one
   midtrain arm (~2 days). LongDoc already controls length and domain; this
   would additionally control vocabulary and structure.
6. **Error analysis by rule type and negation**, from stored per-item
   predictions. Which items flip, and do they concentrate on items needing
   more inference steps or negation handling?
7. **Probes / t-SNE: my recommendation is to skip.** Referees usually read
   t-SNE as decorative, and a linear probe would be a large new experiment
   whose result is hard to tie to the benchmark claim. The item-level and
   control-based evidence above is stronger per unit of effort. Revisit only
   if a referee explicitly asks for mechanism.
